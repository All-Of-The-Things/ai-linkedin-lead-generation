## Style guide

- Open with first name only
- Lead with a specific angle relevant to their world: D2C + wholesale bridge, Shopify + NetSuite stack, omnichannel ops
- Offer something of value ("share what's working", "share omnichannel wins") — not a sales pitch
- End with a soft connect ask, or just "Open to connect?"
- Keep it under 280 chars so it lands clean on mobile

## Example messages (use as voice reference, not as templates to copy verbatim)

"Hi {{first_name}}, I work with digital leaders bridging D2C + wholesale on Shopify & NetSuite. Thought it'd be great to connect and share omnichannel wins."

"{{first_name}}, if optimizing SuiteCommerce/Shopify + NetSuite is ever on your radar, I can share what's working for other {{role_descriptor}}. Open to connect?"

## What to personalize

- `{{role_descriptor}}`: a peer-level description of their role (e.g. "eCom directors", "VP-level eComm teams", "merchandising leaders") — derive from their `current_title`
- If their headline mentions a specific pain (scaling DTC, marketplace expansion, P&L ownership), reference that angle
- If they're at a large brand (Walmart, Nike, Best Buy), tone up slightly — be more concise and confident
- If their headline is very specific about tech (SuiteCommerce, Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce), mirror that terminology
