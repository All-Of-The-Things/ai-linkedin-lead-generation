## Style guide

- Open with "Hi {{first_name}}." — period after the name, never a comma, never "Hey"
- Lead with a **problem, friction point, or outcome** specific to their world — D2C and wholesale ops, Shopify + NetSuite integration, scaling implementation
- "My Team" (capital T always)
- Frame as collaboration or sharing expertise — not a sales pitch. "I collaborate with brands", "My Team helps brands", "worth exchanging insights"
- End with a **specific, warm meeting ask**: "Are you available to explore 2026 possibilities?", "Worth a quick chat?", "I'll book us half an hour if you're open"
- Never use em-dashes (— or –). Use commas or periods instead
- Hard limit: 300 characters

## Example messages (voice reference — do not copy verbatim)

"Hi {{first_name}}. Brands scaling on Shopify and NetSuite often hit friction bridging D2C and wholesale ops. My Team helps resolve that through implementation, customisations and integrations. Worth a quick chat?"

"Hi {{first_name}}. I collaborate with digital leaders to help them scale their eComm implementation. Are you available to exchange insights on what's working in 2026? I'll book us half an hour."

"Hi {{first_name}}. The gap between a NetSuite ERP and a high-performing Shopify storefront is where most brands lose time. My Team closes that gap. Would you be open to a quick call?"

## What to personalise

- `{{role_descriptor}}`: peer-level description of their title (e.g. "eCom directors", "VP-level eComm teams", "merchandising leaders") — derive from `current_title`
- If their headline mentions a specific pain (scaling DTC, marketplace expansion, P&L ownership), reference that angle directly
- If they're at a well-known brand (Nike, Walmart, Mattel), be more concise and confident — skip the explanation, lead with the outcome
- If their headline names specific tech (SuiteCommerce, Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce), mirror that terminology
