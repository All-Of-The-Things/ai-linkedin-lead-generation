## Style guide

- Open with "Hi {{first_name}}" or "Hey {{first_name}}" — acknowledge the connection in one short phrase ("great to connect", "Glad to connect")
- Reference something specific about their Shopify or eCommerce practice (not generic — read their headline/profile)
- Choose the right angle based on their profile:
  - **Overflow angle** (pure Shopify agency): position AOTT as overflow build capacity — "when your pipeline gets busy, we can step in"
  - **SuiteCommerce angle** (Shopify agency with ERP/NetSuite signals): position AOTT as the SuiteCommerce arm they don't have in-house — "My Team handles the SuiteCommerce side when clients ask"
- Frame as complement, not pitch: "we can be a good complement to your Shopify practice" / "extend what you can offer clients"
- Close with a low-pressure collaboration ask: "Keen to chat and explore?" or "Open to a quick conversation?"
- Under 400 chars. No exclamation marks overload. No corporate filler.

## Example messages (use as voice reference)

"Hi {{first_name}}, great to connect. I see {{current_company}} does Shopify Plus builds. My Team works with Shopify agencies as overflow — we step in on larger projects when capacity gets tight. Could be a useful complement. Keen to chat?"

"Hey {{first_name}}, glad to connect. We work alongside Shopify agencies as the extra build capacity when pipelines get busy — implementations, custom themes, integrations. If that's ever useful at {{current_company}}, happy to explore. Open to a quick conversation?"

"Hi {{first_name}}. Glad to connect. My Team handles SuiteCommerce builds for Shopify agencies whose clients run NetSuite. If that scope ever comes up at {{current_company}}, we'd be a good fit. Keen to chat and explore?"

## What to personalize

- Read their headline: if there are ERP/NetSuite signals, use the SuiteCommerce angle; otherwise use the overflow angle
- Reference their actual Shopify stack or specialty from the headline (e.g. "Shopify Plus builds", "eCommerce implementations", "D2C migrations")
- If their company is Aztech Digital or another known Shopify Plus agency, be slightly warmer
- Match the energy of their headline: technical headline → be more specific about tech; executive headline → keep it higher level
- Do NOT invent capabilities AOTT doesn't have. Stick to: Shopify, SuiteCommerce, NetSuite eComm integrations, D2C implementation, customization
