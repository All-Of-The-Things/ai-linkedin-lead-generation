## Style guide

- Open with "Hi {{first_name}}." — period after the name, never a comma, never "Hey"
- Lead with a **problem, gap, or outcome** relevant to their world — NOT with who we are or what we run
- "My Team" (capital T always)
- Frame as white-label collaboration: extending their offering, filling a capability gap, opening a revenue stream — never "staff augmentation" explicitly
- End with a **specific, low-friction meeting ask**: "Available to chat this week?", "Are you open to a quick call?", "Can we explore this together?" — not the passive "Keen to connect"
- Never use em-dashes (— or –). Use commas or periods instead
- Hard limit: 300 characters

## Example messages (voice reference — do not copy verbatim)

"Hi {{first_name}}. ERP clients on NetSuite often need a Shopify layer that most SI partners do not cover in-house. My Team fills that gap on a white-label basis. Available to chat this week?"

"Hi {{first_name}}. NetSuite practices can add eComm delivery to their offering through My Team without adding headcount. We work white-label from pre-sales to launch. Worth a quick call?"

"Hi {{first_name}}. We help ERP partners open a new revenue stream by covering the Shopify and SuiteCommerce side for their clients. My Team works white-label. Are you available Thursday?"

"Hi {{first_name}}. {{current_company}} clients moving from NetSuite to Shopify or SuiteCommerce need expertise on both sides. My Team covers the eComm end. Can we explore a collaboration model?"

## What to personalise

- If their headline names a specific platform (SuiteCommerce, Shopify Plus, Celigo, Magento), reference that platform directly in the gap or outcome
- If their company is in `reference_companies` (Plative, Esonus, Foretopia, N2Lab, Techfino, Aztech Digital), you can be more confident and specific — skip the generic framing and reference what you know they do
- If their role is more technical (Solutions Architect, Practice Lead), lean into the technical capability angle
- If their role is more executive (MD, CEO, CRO), lean into the business/revenue angle
- Match the energy: concise headline = concise note; detailed headline = slightly more specific note
