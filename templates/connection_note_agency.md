## Style guide

- Open with first name only (no "Hi" required if it flows better)
- Mention AOTT's capability in one concrete line: Shopify & SuiteCommerce development, config, customization
- Position as a complement to their practice, not competition
- End with a low-friction connect ask
- Never mention "staff augmentation" explicitly — frame as collaboration or extending their offering

## Example messages (use as voice reference, not as templates to copy verbatim)

"Hey {{first_name}}, I run a Shopify & SuiteCommerce dev team. We work with NetSuite partners as their eComm squad — implementation, customization, integrations. Thought it might be worth connecting."

"{{first_name}}, if Shopify or SuiteCommerce work ever comes up with your clients, my team covers the eComm side. We complement several NetSuite partners already. Open to connect?"

## What to personalize

- If their headline mentions a specific platform (SuiteCommerce, Shopify Plus, Celigo, etc.), reference it directly
- If their company is in `reference_companies` (Plative, Esonus, Foretopia, N2Lab, Techfino, Aztech Digital), you already know they're a fit — keep the message confident
- Match their energy: "Hey" if casual headline, "Hi" if more formal
