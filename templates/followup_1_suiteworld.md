- **Segment A (partner/SI)**: implementation, consulting, SI, VAR, agency, professional services → Partner variant

## Style guide (both variants)

- Open with `"Hi {{first_name}}"` + a short, genuine connection acknowledgment (one phrase, not a sentence)
- Reference something specific from their profile — their stack, their company's delivery model, their role's likely challenge. Never be generic ("I see you work in eCommerce")
- Position AOTT's value in 1–2 sentences. Be concrete about what we do: Shopify development, SuiteCommerce builds, NetSuite-to-Shopify migrations, custom integrations
- Close with a specific **SuiteWorld meeting ask** — this is the primary CTA. Rotate these phrases (never repeat in a batch):
  - `"Would love to grab a coffee at SuiteWorld if you're heading to Vegas."`
  - `"Are you heading to SuiteWorld? Worth finding 20 minutes at the conference."`
  - `"Let's find time in Vegas if you'll be at SuiteWorld."`
  - `"If you're going to SuiteWorld, I'd love to find 20 minutes."`
  - `"Would love to meet up at SuiteWorld — are you heading to Vegas?"`
- Under 400 characters. No exclamation overload. No corporate filler ("touching base", "I wanted to follow up", "hope this finds you well").

**Goal**: get to a specific conversation about how AOTT fits as their delivery arm. Reference their company's delivery model or client base, name the tech stack, and ground the ask in the event.

**Structure**: brief connection ack → specific reference to their offering → AOTT as the eComm complement → SuiteWorld meeting ask

**Example messages (voice reference — do not copy verbatim):**

"Hi {{first_name}}, great to connect. I see {{current_company}} handles NetSuite implementations — My Team covers the Shopify and SuiteCommerce side, so there's a natural fit when client scope pushes into eCommerce. Would love to grab a coffee at SuiteWorld if you're heading to Vegas."

"Hi {{first_name}}. Glad to connect. {{current_company}}'s ERP practice and our eComm build capability tend to be complementary — we step in on the Shopify or SuiteCommerce projects that sit outside the SI's core. Are you heading to SuiteWorld? Worth finding 20 minutes at the conference."

"Hi {{first_name}}, good to connect. When {{current_company}}'s NetSuite clients push into SuiteCommerce or Shopify Plus, that's often where we come in as the delivery arm. Let's find time in Vegas if you'll be at SuiteWorld."

## What to personalise

- **Segment A**: reference the lead's actual delivery area if visible (NetSuite implementations, SuiteCloud development, Shopify Plus delivery). If it's a known SI from the reference list, you can be slightly warmer.
- **Segment B**: name the platform situation you can infer (SuiteCommerce storefront, NetSuite-to-Shopify migration, custom connector). If their role is VP Ops, lean into the operational efficiency angle; if IT Director, lean into the technical integration angle.
- **Do not invent capabilities**: AOTT builds Shopify, SuiteCommerce, and NetSuite eCommerce integrations. Do not reference CRM, ERP configuration, or anything outside that stack.
- If the connection was accepted fast (under 24 hours), the lead is warm — you can be slightly more direct about the meeting ask.
