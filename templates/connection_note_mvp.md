## Style guide

- Open with "Hi {{first_name}}." — period after the name, never a comma, never "Hey"
- Lead with a **specific outcome or pain** relevant to their situation — NOT with who AOTT is or what we run
- "My Team" (capital T always)
- Frame as a **build-partner**, not a dev shop or agency: speed, Spec-driven process (Spec capital S), AI-assisted development
- The core angle: most internal tools don't start with a product brief. They start with someone doing repetitive work and asking why it isn't automated yet. AOTT turns that recognition into a working product.
- Reference proof-of-work products when relevant: Scorestack (sales/RevOps tooling), StoreSync (Shopify env promotion), Bridge (NetSuite-to-Shopify migration). Only mention one, only if it's clearly relevant to their world.
- End with a **specific, low-friction ask**: "Worth a quick call?", "Open to a chat this week?", "Available Thursday?" — not the passive "keen to connect"
- Never use em-dashes (— or –). Use commas or periods instead
- Hard limit: 300 characters

## Two buyer types — personalise to the right one

### Type A: Founder / entrepreneur building a commercial product
- They have an idea or early spec; they need someone to build it fast and predictably
- Angle: speed from Spec to working product, AI-assisted process, no scope drift, no long agency cycles
- The Spec is the contract — out-of-scope is defined upfront, so there are no mid-sprint surprises
- Do NOT mention "staff augmentation" or "white-label" — this is a product-build partnership

### Type B: Company leader needing an internal tool
- They have a repeating manual process — spreadsheets, copy-paste workflows, tribal knowledge held by one person
- They know it should be automated but there is no internal team and no bandwidth to spec it properly
- Angle: AOTT turns that repeating pattern into a working internal tool quickly, Spec-first, without them hiring a team
- Proof-of-work: Bridge and Scorestack started exactly this way — a manual process AOTT was running internally that became a product

## What to personalise

- If their headline or experience mentions a specific pain (manual process, spreadsheets, env drift, data migration, repetitive work, "doing it manually"), reference that pain directly
- If they are in the Shopify or NetSuite space, you can reference StoreSync or Bridge as proof-of-work
- If they are in sales / RevOps, reference Scorestack
- If their role is technical (CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Engineering), lean into the Spec-driven methodology angle: Spec first, agentic execution, human validation — no scope drift
- If their role is business/ops (CEO, COO, Head of Operations), lead with the outcome and skip the methodology detail
- Founders: acknowledge the stage they're in ("building", "launching", "pre-product") and meet them there
- If they mention scale problems ("doing this manually for 50 clients", "repeating the same process", "this doesn't scale"), use that exact language back at them — they've already identified the problem
