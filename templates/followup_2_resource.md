## Style guide

- No greeting ceremony — get to the value in the first line
- Share one concrete, specific insight or observation (not a link to a blog post)
- Make it feel like something you noticed and thought of them, not a mass message
- Zero hard ask. "Let me know if useful" or "happy to go deeper if relevant" is the softest possible close
- Under 350 chars.

## Insight angles by persona (pick the most relevant one)

**Agency / SI Partners:**
- A pattern you've seen in how top NetSuite partners structure their eComm delivery (e.g. keeping Shopify config in-house vs. outsourcing customization)
- A common SuiteCommerce integration mistake that costs project margin
- Something about Shopify B2B or Markets that's becoming a client ask from retail brands

**Retail / eCommerce Brands:**
- A D2C + wholesale sync approach on Shopify + NetSuite that's working well (e.g. channel-specific pricing, inventory allocation)
- A Shopify Markets or B2B portal pattern that reduces ops overhead
- A SuiteCommerce performance tweak that meaningfully moves conversion

## Example messages (use as voice reference)

"Hi {{first_name}}, something we keep seeing: brands running Shopify and NetSuite often hit friction at the order routing layer when scaling wholesale. Happy to share how a few teams solved it. Let me know if that's useful."

"Hi {{first_name}}, one thing NetSuite partners often overlook: scoping Shopify customization separately from SuiteCommerce config saves real margin on delivery. Happy to go deeper if it's relevant to {{current_company}}'s projects."

## What to personalize

- The specific insight must be relevant to their actual role and company — don't use the agency insight for a retail brand lead or vice versa
- Do not fabricate data or statistics. Describe patterns, not numbers.
- The insight should feel like something Martin would genuinely notice and share — operational, specific, not marketing fluff
