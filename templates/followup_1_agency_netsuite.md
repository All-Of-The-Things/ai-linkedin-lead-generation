## Style guide

- Open with "Hi {{first_name}}" or "Hey {{first_name}}" — acknowledge the connection in one short phrase ("great to connect", "Glad to connect")
- Reference something specific about their company's ERP offering or space (not generic — read their headline/profile)
- Position AOTT's value: My Team handles the Shopify and SuiteCommerce side — implementation, customization, integrations
- Frame as complement, not pitch: "we can be a great complement to your NetSuite practice" / "extend your eComm offering for ERP clients"
- Close with a low-pressure collaboration ask: "Keen to chat and explore?" or "Keen to explore if we can complement your capabilities?"
- Under 400 chars. No exclamation marks overload. No corporate filler.

## Example messages (use as voice reference)

"Hi {{first_name}}, great to connect. I see {{current_company}}'s offering includes eCommerce integrations. My Team handles the Shopify side: config, customization, and ongoing dev. Think we could be a solid complement to your NetSuite practice. Keen to chat and explore?"

"Hi {{first_name}}. Glad to connect. My Team works with NetSuite partners as their eComm squad, covering Shopify and SuiteCommerce development and implementation. Keen to explore if we can complement your ERP capabilities?"

"Hey {{first_name}}, great to connect. We work with ERP consultancies as the eCommerce build arm for their clients — Shopify Plus and SuiteCommerce. If that scope ever comes up at {{current_company}}, we'd be a good fit. Open to a quick chat?"

## What to personalize

- Reference their actual ERP stack from the headline (e.g. "NetSuite implementations", "Oracle consulting", "SuiteCommerce builds", "ERP and CRM solutions")
- If their company is in `reference_companies` (Plative, Esonus, Foretopia, N2Lab, Techfino), you can be slightly warmer
- Match the energy of their headline: technical headline → be more specific about tech; executive headline → keep it higher level
- Do NOT invent capabilities AOTT doesn't have. Stick to: Shopify, SuiteCommerce, NetSuite eComm integrations, D2C implementation, customization
