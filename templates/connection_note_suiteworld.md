## Segment Detection (do this first)

Read `headline`, `current_title`, and `current_company`. Classify the lead:

- **Segment A (partner/SI)**: company or headline contains implementation, consulting, SI, VAR, Oracle partner, NetSuite partner, agency, professional services, or similar. Use the **Partner variant** below.
- **Segment B (end-user brand)**: company is a retailer, manufacturer, distributor, consumer brand, or the lead's role is IT Director / VP Operations / Head of eCommerce at a non-agency company. Use the **End-user variant** below.
- When ambiguous, default to Segment A.

---

## Style guide (both variants)

- Open with `"Hi {{first_name}}."` — period after the name. Never a comma, never "Hey"
- Use SuiteWorld as the meeting hook — weave it in naturally, not as a cold opener. It should feel like you're already planning to be at the same event, not like you looked them up to invite them somewhere
- **Hard limit: 300 characters** — the event hook costs ~30 chars, so every word before it must earn its place
- Em-dash allowed once per note as a structural separator
- **Rotate the event phrase** — never use the same one twice in a batch:
  - `"Heading to SuiteWorld this October?"`, `"Going to SuiteWorld?"`, `"Planning to be at SuiteWorld?"`, `"Heading to Vegas for SuiteWorld?"`
- **Rotate the meeting close** — never use the same one twice in a batch:
  - `"Would love to connect in Vegas."`, `"Worth grabbing 20 minutes in Vegas?"`, `"Would love to find time in Vegas."`, `"Let's connect in Las Vegas."`, `"Love to meet up at the event."`
- No exclamation marks. No "I wanted to reach out." No "I came across your profile."

## Variant A — Partner / SI

**Pitch**: delivery capacity. The partner can sell or deliver more without hiring. AOTT handles Shopify and SuiteCommerce builds under their banner.

**Structure**: gap or outcome relevant to their delivery → short AOTT positioning → SuiteWorld meeting ask

**Vocabulary bank** (vary — never repeat):
- Relationship: "your delivery arm", "under your banner", "an extension of your team", "the eComm build layer", "your dedicated eComm squad", "we embed for those projects"
- Capacity: "absorb that overflow", "bolt-on eComm capacity", "you keep the account — we handle the build", "specialist overflow", "more output, same headcount on your end"
- Outcome: "client gets the full stack", "seamless to your client", "you stay on the ERP side, they get a full-service experience"

**Example messages (voice reference — do not copy verbatim):**

"Hi {{first_name}}. SuiteCommerce and Shopify Plus builds are the overflow most NetSuite SIs don't want to staff for permanently. We embed as the eComm squad — you keep the relationship, we handle the build. Heading to SuiteWorld? Love to meet up."

"Hi {{first_name}}. When {{current_company}}'s clients need Shopify or SuiteCommerce built alongside the ERP, that scope usually sits outside what you want on your bench full-time. We step in as your delivery arm. Going to SuiteWorld? Would love to connect in Vegas."

"Hi {{first_name}}. Most NetSuite partners hit a ceiling when client scope pushes into Shopify Plus or SuiteCommerce customisation. We absorb that overflow — you keep the account, the client gets a clean delivery. Planning to be at SuiteWorld? Worth 20 minutes in Vegas?"

## Variant B — End-User Brand

**Pitch**: expert build execution. AOTT builds and customizes SuiteCommerce and Shopify so their team doesn't have to staff for it.

**Structure**: a concrete challenge in their world (ERP-commerce gap, SuiteCommerce debt, Shopify integration) → AOTT as the build layer → SuiteWorld meeting ask

**Vocabulary bank** (vary):
- Positioning: "we build and customise SuiteCommerce", "we own that eComm build layer", "your team stays focused on the business — we handle the technical complexity", "we take on the Shopify/SuiteCommerce side"
- Outcome: "cleaner commerce stack", "storefront that actually reflects your ops", "removes the gap between NetSuite and what your customers see"

**Example messages (voice reference — do not copy verbatim):**

"Hi {{first_name}}. The gap between NetSuite and what a customer sees on a SuiteCommerce storefront is usually where the friction lives. We close that — builds, customisations, integrations. Heading to SuiteWorld? Would love to find time in Vegas."

"Hi {{first_name}}. Most teams running NetSuite hit a point where the SuiteCommerce side needs a dedicated build partner, not another internal hire. That's where we come in. Going to SuiteWorld this October? Worth a quick chat in Vegas."

## What to personalise

- **Segment A**: name the lead's actual stack if it's visible (SuiteCommerce, Shopify Plus, Celigo). If it's a known SI (Plative, Esonus, Techfino, etc.), skip generic framing and reference what their clients encounter.
- **Segment B**: reference their industry or platform situation if visible (e.g. "running SuiteCommerce on a high-SKU catalogue", "NetSuite-to-Shopify migration"). If their headline shows they're mid-implementation, lead with that.
- Match the energy of their headline: sparse headline → sparse note; detailed headline → slightly more specific note.
- Do NOT mention AOTT by name in the connection note — the note is too short and it reads like a pitch deck. Let the framing do the work.
