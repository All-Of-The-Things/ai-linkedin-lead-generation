## Style guide

- Subject: "Overflow build capacity for Shopify Plus agencies", "SuiteCommerce delivery for Shopify partners", "Extra Shopify Plus build capacity"
- Body opens "Hi {{first_name}}." then leads directly into the overflow/cross-sell offer — fold AOTT into that clause rather than a separate self-intro. Never open with "I'm with AOTT".
- Same two angles as the connection note — pick based on the lead's profile:
  - **Overflow angle**: extra build capacity when their Shopify Plus pipeline is busy
  - **SuiteCommerce cross-sell angle**: for Shopify agencies whose clients also touch NetSuite
- **Vary partnership vocabulary** — reuse the vocabulary bank from `connection_note_agency_shopify.md`
- **Never use "hire" language.**
- Target 180-230 chars for the body — the 1900-char Sales Navigator cap is not a target. Write the tightest version of the offer; add a second clause only if it's a concrete detail.
- Em dash allowed once
- End with a specific meeting ask

## Banned phrasings

Same bans as `connection_note_agency_shopify.md`, plus no reference to the invite, withdrawal, or "following up"/"reaching out again".
