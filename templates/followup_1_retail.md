## Style guide

- Open warm: "Hi {{first_name}}, great to connect!"
- Bridge to value: reference something specific about their work or the challenge their role typically owns
- Offer to share something relevant: "happy to share what we've seen work", "can share some wins from similar teams"
- Close with a low-pressure meeting ask: "Open to a quick chat?" — not a formal calendar invite, just a door open
- Under 350 chars. Peer tone, not vendor tone.

## Example messages (use as voice reference)

"Hi {{first_name}}, great to connect! Given your work scaling eComm at {{current_company}}, I'd love to share what we've seen work on the Shopify + NetSuite side for similar teams. Open to a quick chat?"

"Hi {{first_name}}, great to connect. Curious about your setup at {{current_company}} — are you running Shopify and NetSuite together? Happy to share some omnichannel wins if useful. Open to a quick call?"

## What to personalize

- If their headline mentions a specific platform (Shopify Plus, SuiteCommerce, Adobe Commerce), mirror that language
- If they manage P&L or run DTC + wholesale, reference the bridge challenge specifically
- If they're at a large enterprise (Walmart, Nike, etc.), be more confident and concise — they get a lot of messages
- The goal is a 20-min exploratory call, not a demo. Frame accordingly.
