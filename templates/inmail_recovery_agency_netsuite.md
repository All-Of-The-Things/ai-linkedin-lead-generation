## Style guide

- Subject: short, states the offer — "eComm build layer for NetSuite SIs", "Shopify/SuiteCommerce delivery for [current_company]", "On-demand eComm capacity for ERP partners"
- Body opens "Hi {{first_name}}." then leads directly into the outcome the partner gains — fold AOTT into that clause ("We help...", "My Team at AOTT enables...") rather than a separate self-intro. Never open with "I'm with AOTT".
- Lead with the **outcome the ERP partner gains** — wider service offering, more delivery capacity, cleaner client experience, without growing headcount
- **Vary partnership vocabulary** — reuse the vocabulary bank from `connection_note_agency_netsuite.md` (relationship / capacity / service offering / outcome framing)
- **Never use "hire" language.** "Without growing headcount" is fine.
- "White-label" at most once
- Em dash allowed once, as structural separator
- Target 180-230 chars for the body — 1900 is the hard cap, not a target. If there's room for one concrete detail (their likely ERP stack, delivery model), use it in place of a second sentence, not in addition to it.
- End with a specific meeting ask, same rotation as the connection note

## Banned phrasings

Same bans as `connection_note_agency_netsuite.md`, plus no reference to the invite, withdrawal, or "reaching out again"/"following up".
